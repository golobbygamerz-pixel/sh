For My ❤️

Ek random 4 AM conversation se shuru hui humari story…

Shrejal,

Kabhi socha nahi tha ki ek random Omegle chat meri life ki itni special memory ban jayegi.

Main kaafi time se single tha, phir meri sister ne suggest kiya ki Omegle try kar, toh maine bhi socha chalo try karte hain.

Aur phir subah ke 4 baje tum aur Aditi meri video chat mein aaye. Hum teeno kaafi der tak baat karte rahe aur pata hi nahi chala kab itni achhi vibe match ho gayi.

Funny baat ye thi ki hum dono ne ek dusre ki ID bhi nahi maangi, phir jab main jaane laga toh Aditi ne meri ID maangi. Uske baad tumne message kiya aur wahin se humari chatting start hui.

Shayad tumhe laga hoga ki Omegle par mila hai, kuch din baat karega aur phir block kar dega, but mere intentions kabhi aise nahi the. Maine toh tumhe uss din theek se dekha bhi nahi tha, phir bhi tumse baat karna achha lagne laga tha.

Aur sabse cute coincidence, jis din hum mile woh tumhara birthday tha, 19 October. ❤️

Phir Snapchat se Instagram aur Instagram se video calls tak ka safar shuru hua. Pata hi nahi chala kab hum 6+ hours baat karne lage aur kab tum meri daily routine ka favourite part ban gayi.

Mujhe pata hai tum MBBS ki preparation kar rahi ho aur main genuinely chahta hoon ki tum apne saare dreams achieve karo. Main hamesha tumhari mehnat aur success support karna chahta hoon. Kabhi nahi chahta ki meri wajah se tumhari studies ya dreams affect hon.

Main tumhare saath aur late-night conversations, random jokes aur bahut saari memories banana chahta hoon. Ek din tumse real life mein milna hai aur in sab moments ko screen ke bahar bhi experience karna hai.

I don’t know humari story aage kahan tak jayegi, but mere intentions genuinely serious hain aur main is connection ko ek beautiful chance dena chahta hoon.

Thank you meri life mein aane ke liye aur itni saari beautiful memories dene ke liye.

Ek random 4 AM chat se lekar 6+ hours ki calls tak, I genuinely love how our story started.

Maybe humari meeting random thi, but tum mere liye random nahi reh gayi.

I Really Love You, Shrejal. ❤️

With lots of love,

Rihan 💓
